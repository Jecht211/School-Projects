Project Overview: Data Analysis & School Dataset Audit

This project showcases the application of advanced data visualization and analysis techniques learned throughout the course by exploring a 27-graph suite. While the primary goal was to uncover business insights, the analysis ultimately revealed that the school-provided dataset consists entirely of synthetic, randomly generated data rather than real-world sales metrics.
Despite the lack of authentic business correlations, the dataset served as a perfect playground to build an interactive auditing pipeline that statistically proves the data's artificial nature.

Key Features & Findings

• Data Authenticity Audit: Created a data-driven "Story" demonstrating that the dataset is mathematically simulated and unsuitable for actual sales member performance evaluations.
• Randomness Verification:
	• Sales & Profit Uniformity: A horizontal bar chart of sales profit vs. sales by company (in descending order) shows an identical, uniform profit-to-sales linear relationship across all companies and items.
	• Uniform Turnaround Distributions: A histogram of turnaround times (ranging from 2 to 28 days) shows an unnaturally flat distribution, with all bins hovering uniformly around a mean of 295 (ranging strictly between 275 and 321).
	• Identical Box & Whisker Profiles: Turnaround times per item yielded zero outliers, identical ranges, and nearly identical averages across the board—a mathematical impossibility in real business operations.
• Interactive Dynamic Dashboards: Built a fully interactive dashboard allowing users to filter sales goals, turnaround times, profits, and regions dynamically by Year and Sales Member.
